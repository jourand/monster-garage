The Hâpax Difference

We Chose Epoxy Because It's Better — Not Because It's Faster.

A lot of coating companies push polyurea because it lets them finish and leave in one day. That speed comes from a thinner, faster-curing system. We install true epoxy because it penetrates and chemically bonds with the concrete itself — resulting in a harder, more repairable floor that's built to be lived on for years, not just shown off on install day.

Quick Fix

Big-Box Paint Kits

  • Surface-level adhesion only
  • Peels and lifts within 1-2 years
  • No real surface prep
  • Cheapest option, shortest life
Hâpax Standard

Industrial Epoxy System

  • Diamond-ground surface prep for real penetration
  • Chemically bonds and seals into the slab
  • Higher build thickness = more impact resistance
  • Fully repairable and recoatable over time
  • Torginol flake color systems for a decorative, durable finish
The Trade-Off

Polyurea "1-Day" Systems

  • Faster install, thinner build
  • Optimized for speed, not depth of bond
  • Often marketed on cure time alone
  • A few extra days of cure buys a meaningfully stronger seal

Frequently Asked Questions

Why epoxy instead of polyurea?

Polyurea's main selling point is a one-day install. Our epoxy systems take a bit longer to cure, but they penetrate and chemically bond into the concrete for a harder, more repairable floor. We think the extra day or two of cure time is worth the trade for a floor built to last, not just to look good on install day.

How long until I can use my garage again?

Timelines depend on the specific system and conditions, but most epoxy floors support foot traffic within about 24 hours and vehicle traffic within a few days. We'll walk you through the exact timeline for your project at your free on-site quote.

What areas do you serve?

We install throughout Hampton Roads and up to Richmond, including Norfolk, Virginia Beach, Chesapeake, Hampton, Newport News, Williamsburg, and the Peninsula.

Do you offer storage or cabinets yet?

Not yet — we're launching focused on garage floor coatings first. Storage and cabinet solutions are planned as we grow. Get in touch and we'll let you know when that's available.

What happens at the free quote appointment?

We come out, look at your existing concrete, take measurements, and talk through color and finish options. You'll leave with a clear, no-obligation price for your project.
